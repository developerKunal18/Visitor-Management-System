Features
- Register new visitors with their name, phone number, purpose, and person to meet.
- Automatically generate visitor IDs.
- Record check-in and check-out times.
- Search visitors by name or visitor ID.
- View currently checked-in visitors.
- Display daily visitor reports.
- Store records locally in JSON files.
- Use a menu-driven command-line interface.
